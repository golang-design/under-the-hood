This chapter explains how Go came to have its present shape. It begins with the engineering problems
Google faced, what each of the three designers brought, and why Go deliberately offers fewer
features (1.1). It then describes the three parts of Go, the language, the runtime and the
toolchain, and the unusual decisions made in each (1.2). Finally it traces the ideas behind
goroutines and channels from a 1978 paper to Go's design (1.3).
